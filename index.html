About

Hi!! I'm a trilingual Brazilian super passionate about the intersection between business and technology. A decade of experience in software programming and multimedia production, particularly in game development and video/broadcast, has given me a customer-centric mindset and engineering hard skills. I specialize in bridging the gap between technical and humanistic fields to leverage a team's skillsets as greater than the sum of their parts.

I am currently a student at Boston University pursuing a dual major in Business Administration & Psychology, minor in Computer Science, and concentrations in Information Systems and Marketing. Between semesters abroad in Sydney and Milan and working across colleges at BU, I've had the honor of studying at incredibly diverse institutions that have led me to reaffirm my commitment to people-centric work in tech and business.

Open to roles in product management, consulting, software engineering, marketing, game design and adjacent fields. Always happy to make new connections!
