About

Hey all!

I was born in Brazil, grew up in Chile, and live in the U.S where I'm super passionate about the intersection between business and technology. I grew up hooked on coding as a tool for creation, always thinking I would end up as a computer scientist or engineer. For years I participated in video game design competitions and hackathons, completed programming projects and produced technical events. More importantly, I had the privilege of leading people wildly different from myself and each other. I saw how much more than just code went into making our games and productions and I fell in love, not with the commits we were pushing to the repo, but with the passion and diversity in our team. Thats when I knew I do love tech, but above that I love people, and I've strived to work in high-performing teams ever since.

Im currently at Boston University pursuing a 5-year dual major in Business Administration and Psychology, with concentrations in Information Systems and Marketing, and a minor in Computer Science. In the future I'm looking forward to studying abroad in Sydney and Milan, working with Questrom's IT department, and continuing to grow as a person, student, and professional. I'm hungry to learn, not afraid to fail, and try to seize every opportunity life gives me. Dont hesitate to reach out!
